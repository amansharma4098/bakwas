Our methodology

A problem for every solution.

A proven three-step framework, refined over 100+ enterprise engagements.

01

Find a solved problem

We look for enterprises whose processes already work well. Those are the easiest to improve, by which we mean change.

02

Add AI

We put AI on top of the solution. Then we add a dashboard to monitor the AI, and a second AI to explain the dashboard.

03

Deliver the problem

Congratulations: the client now has a problem. Also an annual contract, an onboarding deck and our CTO's phone number.
